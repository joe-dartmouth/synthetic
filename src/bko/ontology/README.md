# `bko.ontology`: ontology loading and field mapping

Load MONDO, HPO and DO from pinned OBO releases with pronto, and map their concepts to dataset fields, starting with the disease label and chest-pain type. Each mapping records its source, encoding and limits; numeric fields are governed by separately specified range constraints, defined with their frame in `bko.datasets` and re-exported by `bko.ontology.mapping`; concepts with no defensible field are out of scope, and the near misses are listed with their reasons. The same table maps Synthea's SNOMED-CT condition codes to the MONDO concepts the release equates with them, and lists the codes it excludes with their reasons.

The loader returns standard-library values, so no other stage imports pronto. The releases are pinned in `bko.datasets.sources`, and the rows are typed constants in `bko.ontology.mapping`. ADR [`research/ontology-mapping-design`](../../../docs/adr/research/ontology-mapping-design.md) records the design, and [`docs/DEVELOPMENT.md`](../../../docs/DEVELOPMENT.md#ontologies) the command that checks the table against the releases. No row has had clinician review, which the plan makes conditional on availability ([RESEARCH, Represent](../../../docs/RESEARCH.md#represent)). Loaded releases convert to BKB fragments in `bko.ontology.fragments` (ADR [`research/bko-fragment-encoding`](../../../docs/adr/research/bko-fragment-encoding.md)). `bko.ontology.fragments.scope_of` gives the classes a release's fragment holds for some mapped concepts, and `bko.ontology.releases.load_releases` fetches and loads the pinned releases a command reads. `bko.ontology.santos` rebuilds Santos et al.'s MONDO/DO fusion example from the pinned releases ([`docs/DEVELOPMENT.md`](../../../docs/DEVELOPMENT.md#fusion-reproduction)).

- **Dependencies:** The `ontology` extra (`pyproject.toml`), `bko.datasets` with its `data` extra, and `bko.bkb`.
