# `bko.evaluate`: evaluation

Rare-class recall and AUC-PR (scikit-learn), fidelity (SDMetrics), privacy as distance to closest record (SDMetrics) and, read from stored fills, as membership inference and NNDR, and the validity rate of generated records.

The harness scores every arm (the baselines and the three BKO arms; in the run `g5`, `bko` and the prescribing arm `bko-skeleton`, whose every request carries each record's concepts drawn from the fused BKB (`skeleton`); or, in the free run `g2`, the direct arm `bko-direct`, which draws records from the fused BKB with no API call; ADR [`research/bkb-direct-generation`](../../../docs/adr/research/bkb-direct-generation.md)) on E1's repeated holdouts and judges a candidate arm against E1's criteria. The runs `e3`, `e3-a` and `e3-b` score E1's arms on the Synthea frame instead, each holdout's training rows thinned by the experiment's removal before any arm runs, plus the reference arm `real`, which has no runner and adds back the removed rows (ADR [`research/synthea-downstream-design`](../../../docs/adr/research/synthea-downstream-design.md)). Their held-out ratio reads the split before the removal, and their experiment keeps copies (`Experiment.decontaminate` false): a test record an arm copies is counted and stays in the test split, where E1 removes it. Every holdout writes a `flagged-fingerprint` held-out score of its flagged cells, and `beside.pairable` pairs a holdout only when both files' fingerprints match or either lacks one; such a run writes a `copies-kept` held-out row per holdout, and `pairable`'s `allow_copies`, which `compare` sets for an experiment that keeps copies, lets a holdout with a copy pair when both files hold that row there. The runs `b3-uci` and `b3-synthea` add the CLLM arms `cllm` and `cllm-uncurated` beside `bko`: each of their calls shows training records drawn per cell (module `cllm`), the arm `cllm` drops the rows a boosting classifier fit on the training rows confidently puts at the other label (module `curation`), and every paid arm (`experiment.PAID_ARMS`, the Messages arms and `bko-direct-perturbed`) refuses data held under an agreement that grants no permission to send it (ADR [`research/cllm-arm-design`](../../../docs/adr/research/cllm-arm-design.md)); `waves` holds the wave helpers every Messages arm's rounds use. `bko.evaluate.experiment.E1` holds the configuration and `requests` the gap and replica requests every arm is given, which `experiment` re-exports; ADR [`research/e1-evaluation-protocol`](../../../docs/adr/research/e1-evaluation-protocol.md) records the protocol, and [`docs/EVIDENCE.md`](../../../docs/EVIDENCE.md#e1-uci-heart-disease-augmentation) states how each criterion is read. The CLI's `__main__` parses each command, its flags held in `arguments`, and dispatches it, the reader subcommands `shared`, `rows`, `panel`, `privacy`, `truth` and `contradictions` to their handlers in `reader_commands`, and every subcommand reads its named run and the run folders' root through `run_context`; `launch` starts a paid run under `supervise`, which reads each finished segment through `segments`, and a resume's stopped log passes `resumes` before either claims a folder; `context` holds its `Context`, what a command reaches outside the process, `data` holds the `Data` (records, frame, field rows, name, the data use agreement they are held under, and digest) a named run's dataset chooses and every stage of `score` reads, and loads each dataset with its frame from `bko.ontology.frames`, `scoring` holds the `score` path, `run` scores each holdout's arms (each arm's and replica's scores in `run_scores`), each arm's fill retried and its shortfall kept as a partial fill through `filling` and `shortfall`, and `guided_run` holds `GuidedRun` and `guided_arms`, which build a run's BKO arms over one call log and one candidates file, for the evaluate command and the app alike. E9's runs `e9-thin` and `e9-empty`, and their holdout slices `e9-thina` to `e9-thinj` and `e9-emptya` to `e9-emptyj`, score E6's frame and removal with `none`, `real`, `bko-direct` and the logic-only direct arm `bko-logic`, which draws presence from the ontology's constraints alone and reads no data share; every direct arm is built by `run_direct` and read by `run_folder` (ADR [`research/logic-only-ablation`](../../../docs/adr/research/logic-only-ablation.md)). The direct arm `bko-nomap` draws as `bko-direct` does with an empty mapping table, so its contrast with `bko-direct` reads what the table adds; the control runs `e12-thin`, `e12-empty` and `e13` hold it (ADR [`research/no-mapping-ablation`](../../../docs/adr/research/no-mapping-ablation.md)). The direct arm `bko-capped` draws as `bko-direct` does under a donor floor of 2 and a reuse cap of 1 per unit; the runs `e14-thin` and `e14-empty` and their slices `e14-thina` to `e14-thinj` and `e14-emptya` to `e14-emptyj` hold it, with `bko-direct` their privacy reference arm (ADR [`research/sampler-donor-limits`](../../../docs/adr/research/sampler-donor-limits.md)). The direct arm `bko-micro` draws as `bko-capped` does with each numeric field but `age` the mean of three donors; the runs `e20-thin` and `e20-empty` and their slices `e20-thina` to `e20-thinj` and `e20-emptya` to `e20-emptyj` hold it, with `bko-capped` their privacy reference arm, `trace` names each mean's donors under `averaged`, which donor reuse counts, and `attribution` writes a `donor-mean` row for its fills alone (ADR [`research/microaggregated-donors`](../../../docs/adr/research/microaggregated-donors.md)). The direct arm `bko-tree` draws each tree field as one leaf at its ancestor-shrunk share; the runs `e15-thin` and `e15-empty` and their slices `e15-thina` to `e15-thinj` and `e15-emptya` to `e15-emptyj` hold it on `diabetes-codes`, with `bko-direct` their privacy reference arm, every arm of such a run writes `code-share-error` (`run_scores`), and `redraw`'s `TreeShares` gives `trace` each drawn leaf's parts (ADR [`research/ancestor-shrunk-shares`](../../../docs/adr/research/ancestor-shrunk-shares.md)). The direct arms `bko-hier-0`, `bko-hier-w` and `bko-hier-closed` draw each `is_a` pair's broad concept given its narrow one; the runs `e16-thin` and `e16-empty` and their slices hold them on E6's frame, with `bko-direct` their privacy reference arm, and `redraw`'s `Shares` gives `trace` each broad concept's pair parts (ADR [`research/bkb-direct-generation`](../../../docs/adr/research/bkb-direct-generation.md), Decision 12). The direct arm `bko-coherent` (module `coherent`) draws as `bko-direct` does, then redraws its free fields by sequential CART given the cell, the label and the mapped fields (`bko.baselines.conditional`) through `DirectArm.fill`'s `change` hook; the runs `e17-thin` and `e17-empty` and their slices hold it on E6's frame, with `bko-direct` its privacy reference arm, and `trace` and donor reuse re-run its change to name each free field's leaf donor (ADR [`research/free-field-synthesis`](../../../docs/adr/research/free-field-synthesis.md)). The runs `e19-thin`, `e19-empty`, `e19-supported-thin` and `e19-supported-empty` and their slices (`runs_direct`) hold the matched knowledge controls: the pooled direct arms `bko-donor`, `bko-pool-random` and `bko-pool-all`, the knowledge-only arm `bko-kb`, and the retained-data methods `row-resample`, `marginals`, `eb-marginals` and `hb-marginals`, beside `bko-direct`, `bko-nomap`, `bko-logic`, `label-bootstrap` and `prior-shift`, with `real` their privacy reference arm (ADR [`research/matched-knowledge-controls`](../../../docs/adr/research/matched-knowledge-controls.md)); the supported runs score `diabetes-supported`, a variant `data` holds in `VARIANTS` over `diabetes` (ADR [`research/dataset-variants`](../../../docs/adr/research/dataset-variants.md)). `direct_arms` holds every direct arm's name with the draw spec it reads. The control `prior-shift` (module `shift`) adds no rows: `run_scores` scores `none`'s fit with each requested subgroup's test logits moved by the offset that brings its out-of-fold training predictions to the subgroup's prevalence after the gap fill, `panel` regenerates it, and the control runs hold it too (ADR [`research/prior-shift-comparator`](../../../docs/adr/research/prior-shift-comparator.md)). The pilot runs `e10-pilot-thin` and `e10-pilot-empty` add two arms beside `bko-direct` on E6's frame and removal: `bko-direct-perturbed` rewrites the 19 unmapped Diabetes fields of its rows through the Messages API, and `bko-direct-pram` redraws them by PRAM with no API call; each draws through `DirectArm.fill`, whose `change` hook passes the drawn rows to the perturbation. The PRAM-only full runs `e10-pram-thin` and `e10-pram-empty`, and their slices `e10-pram-thina` to `e10-pram-thinj` and `e10-pram-emptya` to `e10-pram-emptyj`, run `none`, `real`, `bko-direct` and `bko-direct-pram` on E6's holdouts with no API call. Each arm keeps its own rows on every holdout, and each holdout logs a reading of them against the label-1 training rows: the column shapes of the perturbed and the drawn rows, their difference, pair trends, each column's shape and, on Diabetes, the share of rows breaking a coherence rule (ADR [`research/identifier-perturbation`](../../../docs/adr/research/identifier-perturbation.md)). `perturb` holds the fields and the reading, `pram` the PRAM arm, `perturbed` the rewrite arm and its merge by `row`, `quotas` each rewrite call's value counts per field, set from the cell's label-group training rows, and `bko.generate.rewrite` the rewrite call. The E5 runs keep a share of each cell's training rows before any arm runs (`Experiment.training_fraction`, ADR [`research/e1-evaluation-protocol`](../../../docs/adr/research/e1-evaluation-protocol.md)), and their held-out ratio reads that subsample. `merge` joins holdout slices' scores into one run's, refusing a slice whose sibling `scores.log` names other data, versions or configuration. `shared` rescores a run from its own folder on the test split that neither it nor a compared run copies, writing a `shared-split` row per holdout (module `shared_split`) that `beside` pairs on, after `run_folder` reads each run folder and checks the folders against each other (ADR [`research/shared-test-split-reading`](../../../docs/adr/research/shared-test-split-reading.md)). `rows` rescores a run's folder at its own models through the same checks and a strict check 7, and writes every fill of every arm to a sha-verified `rows.jsonl` that later readings read in place of regenerating, through `read_rows`, `bind` and `frame_of` in `rows_read` (ADR [`research/rebuilt-rows-store`](../../../docs/adr/research/rebuilt-rows-store.md)). `panel` scores a run's stored fills under `classifier.PANEL`, a random forest, extra trees, a multilayer perceptron and a categorical naive Bayes beside the regression and the boosting model, with each model's Brier score and calibration-in-the-large (`utility`'s `calibration`) and no verdict; it applies `rows`' checks, letting check 2's `models=` list extend the folder's, answers each stored baseline fill through `StoredBaseline`, and runs inside `threadpool_limits(limits=1)` (ADR [`research/classifier-panel`](../../../docs/adr/research/classifier-panel.md)). `likelihood` reads a run's candidates file, or a sliced run's files as one, and asks whether the BKB likelihood (`bko.gaps.likelihood`) separates the `bko` arm's records by fidelity, G7's free gate (ADR [`research/bkb-candidate-curation`](../../../docs/adr/research/bkb-candidate-curation.md)); `ranking` holds `Scorers`, the scorers over one holdout's training rows that the gate and the arm `bko-curated` share, which keeps each call cell's highest-scoring rows of twice its quota and marks the rest `dropped-likelihood`, and `over_ask_error`, the `over_ask` rule every reader of it calls. `privacy` reads a run's stored fills for membership inference, a field-group match attack, the donor reuse of each direct or perturbation fill re-drawn under `trace`'s identity checks, and NNDR beside distance to closest record, through `membership`, the match attack of `donor_attacks`, the scopes and statistics both attacks share in `attack_stats`, the reference arm of `reference`, the bound stores of `privacy_stores` and the distances of `privacy`, with each arm's contrast against the run's reference arm read by `privacy_rule` and no verdict (ADR [`research/membership-inference-reading`](../../../docs/adr/research/membership-inference-reading.md)); it makes no API call. `truth` reads, through the module `truth`, each arm's gap fill of a run with a removal against the records the removal took, re-derived by `prepare`: the arm's rows in the removed cell are scored by the quality report a replica's fidelity is scored by (`fidelity`), the stores bound by `privacy_stores`, and `real`, whose rows are the removed records, left out; it makes no API call (ADR [`research/removed-truth-reading`](../../../docs/adr/research/removed-truth-reading.md)). `trace` re-draws one fill of a direct arm at its logged attempt's seed through `redraw`, rebuilds a perturbation arm's fill from its candidate lines over that re-draw, or reads a Messages arm's facts from the folder's call log through `call_prompts` or a baseline's from its `arm` line, and writes each record's explanation to `trace.jsonl`; it refuses unless the re-drawn fill hashes to the `arm` line's sha256 and its kept records are the candidates file's kept lines, and makes no API call (ADR [`research/record-explainability`](../../../docs/adr/research/record-explainability.md)). `attribution` reads `trace.jsonl` files and counts, per fill, each record field's class (a knowledge fact, a donor mean, a training donor id, a prompt section only, or method and seed only) from each line's arm kind, with a baseline's fill counted once, and makes no API call (the same ADR, decision 7). `contradictions` lists, through `bko.gaps.contradictions`, where each mapped frame's training split at holdout seed 0 contradicts its ontology (UCI's, Synthea's and Diabetes 130's, or the frames a repeatable `--dataset` names), in the five kinds of ADR [`research/data-contradiction-handling`](../../../docs/adr/research/data-contradiction-handling.md), with no API call; `contradictions <run> --rows` counts, through `added_contradictions`, those kinds and the three Diabetes coherence rules of `perturb.rule_breaks` in each fill's added rows of a run's `rows` stores. `syntheval` scores each replica fill of a run's `rows` store under SynthEval's metrics: `syntheval_inputs` reads each fill with its holdout's training split and the test split the run scored and holds the metric set `METRICS`, and `syntheval_reading` writes each fill's inputs to a temporary directory and runs `bench/syntheval/score.py` in the environment `make syntheval-env` builds outside the lock, through an injectable runner; it makes no API call, and the run `p2` (`runs_published.P2`) is the run it reads (ADR [`research/syntheval-reading`](../../../docs/adr/research/syntheval-reading.md)). The runs `p3-fifteen` to `p3-ninetyfive` and their slices (`runs_published.P3`) remove Juwara et al.'s fractions of E6's cell, flag that cell by name (`Experiment.flagged_cell`), so the gap request restores it alone, and fill it to `runs_published.juwara_total`; a run with a flagged cell writes each model's `auc-roc` on both scopes (`utility`'s `roc`), and no other run does (ADR [`research/covariate-bias-comparison`](../../../docs/adr/research/covariate-bias-comparison.md)). The runs `e18-two` to `e18-all` and their slices (`runs_direct.E18`) replace a share of each cell's training rows (`Experiment.synthetic_share`): `prepare` names each holdout's dropped rows (`bko.datasets.split.dropped`) and keeps the whole split, `requests.gap_request` asks each cell for its dropped count, so every reader that rebuilds the request from the prepared split gets the same quota, each arm fits on the whole split, each classifier trains on the split less the dropped rows (`prepare.Prepared.kept`) plus the fill (`run_scores`), and `real` adds the dropped rows back; `reference` reads their flagged cell as the cell scope, with `bko-direct` the reference arm (ADR [`research/synthetic-share-ablation`](../../../docs/adr/research/synthetic-share-ablation.md)). `power.mde` is the smallest mean contrast a design's corrected interval reads above zero at a given power, from the contrast's standard deviation, the holdouts and the held-out ratio. The commands are in [`docs/DEVELOPMENT.md`](../../../docs/DEVELOPMENT.md#evaluation), the pilot's `launch` commands among them, except `contradictions`, which is under [Gap detection](../../../docs/DEVELOPMENT.md#gap-detection).

- **Dependencies:** The `eval` extra (`pyproject.toml`), `bko.baselines` and `bko.datasets` with their `synth` and `data` extras, and, for the BKO arms, `bko.gaps`, `bko.validate`, `bko.generate` with its `llm` extra and `bko.ontology` with its `ontology` extra; the direct arm reads `bko.generate.direct` in place of the Messages API.
- **Status:** built.
