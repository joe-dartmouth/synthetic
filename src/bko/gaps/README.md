# `bko.gaps`: gap detection

Find statements the ontology implies but the data does not support. Santos et al. state, as their Proposition 5.1.1, that these ungrounded statements contribute nothing to the model's distribution, so the plan uses them as the formal definition of a dataset gap. Count-based gaps are detected and evaluated separately. `bko.gaps.observe` turns a dataset into fragments, whole and per cell (ADR [`research/dataset-fragment-encoding`](../../../docs/adr/research/dataset-fragment-encoding.md)), `bko.gaps.detect` grounds them and lists count-based gaps, `bko.gaps.definitions` reads a dataset's (cell, statement) gaps under each definition of ADR [`research/bko-formal-gap-definition`](../../../docs/adr/research/bko-formal-gap-definition.md), `bko.gaps.experiment` holds E2's ablations and their reading, `bko.gaps.scan` finds the removed count at which each detector first flags each truth item of a whole ablation, `bko.gaps.sweep` reads E2's ablations and the UCI training split at each grounding count and frequency-aware level and chooses a level by D1's rule, and `bko.gaps.scope` loads the UCI training split, its cells and the pinned releases with their fragments for the commands that read them; `bko.gaps.definitions.completeness_request` builds the completeness request, `bko.gaps.report` holds the report columns the commands share, `bko.gaps.shares` holds each mapped concept's fused share in a cell, which the direct sampler draws at (ADR [`research/bkb-direct-generation`](../../../docs/adr/research/bkb-direct-generation.md)), and `bko.gaps.likelihood` scores a record by the log-probability of that draw making its concepts (ADR [`research/bkb-candidate-curation`](../../../docs/adr/research/bkb-candidate-curation.md)); [`docs/DEVELOPMENT.md`](../../../docs/DEVELOPMENT.md#gap-detection) holds the command. A caller reads a dataset's gaps under one definition with `record_cells` (a training frame) or `population_cells` (a Synthea population), `bko.gaps.detect.ontology_fragments` over the concepts its rows map, then `gaps`.

- **Dependencies:** The `data` extra (`pyproject.toml`), and `bko.ontology` with its `ontology` extra, and `bko.bkb`.
