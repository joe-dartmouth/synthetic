# Evidence register

Sources: [2-pager](../papers/proposal-2-pager.pdf) sections 2, 3 and 5; [Santos et al.](../papers/santos-2024.pdf) section 7 for R1.

The results register: one entry per experiment, reproduction or paid generation run, holding its configuration, its result and what the
result was read against. The criteria live in [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md); an entry links the
section that holds them rather than restating them.

## How to read an entry

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Status | `not yet run`, `running` or `complete` |
| Question | What the experiment answers |
| Configuration | Everything needed to rerun it: dataset and split with the dataset snapshot's identity (download date and checksum; for Synthea its `run.json`: jar checksum, seeds, snapshot date, population size and CSV checksums), the generator's model id, sampling parameters and prompt, baselines and controls, metrics and library versions, seeds, the commit, and how the readings [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md#4-success-criteria) leaves open were fixed |
| Runs | One dated row per run: run id, date, commit, where its logs and artifacts live, and, for runs that call a paid API, its API spend, the model the API reported, its token counts and where its request/response log lives. A rerun adds a row; a superseded or failed run keeps its row with the reason |
| Result | What was measured, with units, and the command that produced it. Empty until the status is `complete` |
| Read against | The RESEARCH.md section whose criteria judge the result, and the verdict: met, not met, inconclusive (its intervals decide neither way), or a null result reported as such |

Classifier: `bko.evaluate.classifier`. How the criteria of [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md#4-success-criteria) are read: primary, the candidate beats every comparator, however slightly, in both recall and AUC-PR over the whole test split, each paired difference's corrected interval lying above zero (`E1`'s margin is zero); secondary, the same over the flagged subgroups, the age-sex subgroups that hold an underrepresented cell; privacy, the 5th percentile of the added rows' distance to the training rows reaches `E1`'s share of the test rows' 5th percentile, its interval lying above it; completeness comes from the fusion stage ([C1](#c1-gap-closure-after-fusion)), not from this harness; validity is `bko`'s pass rate summed over every `bko.validate` call it makes, reported beside its dropped candidates (ADR [`research/e1-evaluation-protocol`](adr/research/e1-evaluation-protocol.md)). A criterion is met when every interval it reads lies above its threshold, not met when one lies below it at the family-wise level (each of its k intervals read at confidence 1 - (1 - c)/k, c being `E1`'s confidence), and inconclusive otherwise. Not fixed: the BKO arms' generator model id, sampling parameters and prompt |
