# Prepare the ICML manuscript and submission materials

The set's graph is [direct-index.md](direct-index.md).

## Why this unit exists

The contribution type and abstract depend on the measured result.

## Scope

Draft the eight-page main paper, impact statement, references and appendix. Tie each claim and plotted point to an evidence entry and a reproduction command. Review limitations, privacy, negative results and cross-domain scope with coauthors. Consider General for a supported methodological advance and Negative Results or Datasets, Evaluations and Benchmarks if the evidence fits.

**Findings from planning.**

- The [official call](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers), as read on 2026-11-26,
  names the track "Datasets, Evaluations and Benchmarks", limits the main paper to 8 pages,
  carries an AI disclosure statement the submission must follow, and sets a reciprocal-reviewer
  rule.

## Acceptance

Coauthors review the blinded file, reproductions, contribution type, substantive generative AI disclosure, submission quotas and reciprocal reviewer eligibility against the official call. Freeze supported abstract claims before the abstract deadline. Record readiness and outstanding limitations. Submission belongs to the authors.
